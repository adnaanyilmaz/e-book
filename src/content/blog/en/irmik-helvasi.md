Nobody in the kitchen can stay patient while semolina helva is toasting. The secret is to take your time and toast the semolina until it turns golden.

## Method

1. Warm the milk, water and sugar in a separate pot until the sugar dissolves. Do not let it boil.
2. Melt the butter in a wide pot and add the semolina and pine nuts.
3. Toast over low heat, stirring constantly, until the semolina turns light brown (about 20 minutes).
4. Carefully pour in the hot syrup, stir and cover.
5. Turn off the heat and let it rest for 15 minutes.
