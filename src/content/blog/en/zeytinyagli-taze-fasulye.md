Olive oil dishes need patience. A low flame and a little time, and the beans do the rest.

## Method

1. Trim the ends of the beans and cut them in half.
2. Gently sauté the sliced onions in olive oil.
3. Add the beans and stir for a few minutes.
4. Add the grated tomatoes, sugar, salt and hot water.
5. Cover and cook over low heat for 45 minutes.
6. Let it cool in the pot. It tastes even better the next day.
