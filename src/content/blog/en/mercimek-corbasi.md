In our house, lentil soup is the first sign of a cold day. The smell of butter and cumin drifting from the kitchen is enough to bring everyone to the table.

## Method

1. Rinse the lentils until the water runs clear.
2. Melt the butter in a pot and sauté the chopped onion until soft.
3. Add the flour and stir until it smells toasty.
4. Add the carrot, potato and lentils, then pour in the hot water.
5. Simmer with the lid ajar for about 30 minutes, until the vegetables are soft.
6. Blend until smooth, season with salt and spices, and bring back to a gentle boil.

## Tip

Drizzle with butter sizzled with chilli flakes before serving, and never forget the lemon.
